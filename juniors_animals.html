Hair, feed young milk, warm-blooded

🧠 The Big Idea

  • Scientists group animals by shared features. The big split: vertebrates (with a backbone) vs invertebrates (without).
  • Vertebrates split into 5 classes: fish, amphibians, reptiles, birds & mammals.
  • Classification helps us study millions of species in an organised way. 🗂️
