🧠 The Big Idea

  • The cell is the basic unit of life. Each organelle is like an organ doing a special job.
  • Plant cells have extras: a stiff cell wall, green chloroplasts (for photosynthesis) and a big central vacuole.
  • The nucleus is the control centre; mitochondria are the powerhouses. ⚡
