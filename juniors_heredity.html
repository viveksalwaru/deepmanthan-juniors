🧠 The Big Idea

  • Genes come in pairs (alleles) — one from each parent. A dominant allele (T) shows even if only one copy is present; a recessive one (t) shows only as tt.
  • Mendel's classic Tt × Tt cross gives the famous 3 : 1 phenotype ratio (3 Tall : 1 short).
  • Variations like these, over many generations, are the raw material of evolution.
