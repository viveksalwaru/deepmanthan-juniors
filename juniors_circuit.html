🧠 The Big Idea

  • Ohm's Law: current I = V ÷ R. More voltage → more current. More resistance → less current.
  • Series: resistances add up (R+R), so current is smaller and bulbs are dimmer. Break one → all go dark (single path).
  • Parallel: each bulb gets the full voltage, so both stay bright. Break one → the other stays lit! (This is how home wiring works. 🏠)
🎯 Try this: Switch to Series and “break” one bulb — both go out. Now switch to Parallel and break one — the other keeps glowing. That's why your whole house doesn't go dark when one bulb fuses!
