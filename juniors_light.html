🧠 The Big Idea

  • Concave mirror & convex lens can make real images (caught on a screen) — usually inverted. Bring the object very close (inside F) → image turns virtual, erect & magnified (that's a shaving mirror / magnifying glass! 🔍).
  • Convex mirror & concave lens always give a virtual, erect, diminished image — a wide view (car rear-view mirror 🚗).
  • Mirror formula: 1/v + 1/u = 1/f  ·  Lens formula: 1/v − 1/u = 1/f  ·  Magnification: m = h′/h
