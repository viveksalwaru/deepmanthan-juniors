🧠 The Big Idea

  • On a circle of radius 1, a point at angle θ sits at (cos θ, sin θ) — cosine is the across-distance, sine is the up-distance.
  • In a right triangle: sin = opposite/hypotenuse, cos = adjacent/hypotenuse, tan = opposite/adjacent.
  • These let us find heights & distances we can't measure directly — like a tower's height from its shadow's angle. 🗼
