🌈 Social Health

Good health is more than not being sick — it's feeling well in body, mind and friendships. Let's explore!

💪
PhysicalA body that's active, rested and cared for.
🧠
MentalFeeling calm, confident and able to handle ups & downs.
🤝
SocialKind friendships and getting along with others.

🌱 Tap the good habits you enjoy — grow your garden!

Every healthy habit counts. 💚

🛟 Feeling stressed? Try these

Breathe slowly
A few deep breaths calm your mind quickly.
Talk to someone
Share worries with a friend, parent or teacher.
Take a break
Step away from screens, move around, play.
Say a strong "no"
To tobacco, alcohol & other addictions — they harm your health.

🧠 The Big Idea

  • Health has three sides: physical, mental and social — they support each other.
  • Small daily habits — rest, movement, kindness, hobbies — build lifelong wellbeing.
  • If you ever feel low or overwhelmed, talking to a trusted adult is a strong, healthy choice. 💬
