🧠 The Big Idea

  • GST (Goods & Services Tax) is added to a price; within a state it splits equally into CGST + SGST.
  • Simple interest: SI = P×R×T/100. Compound interest earns "interest on interest": A = P(1 + R/100)ᵀ.
