🌍 Environmental Management

Everything in nature is connected. Tap a living thing to remove it — and watch the whole food chain feel it!

🍽️ Food Chain — who eats whom

Energy flows from the Sun → plants → animals. Tap any creature to see what happens if it disappears.

⚡ Energy Pyramid — only ~10% passes up

Each level keeps only ~10% of the energy — that's why top predators are few.

🧠 The Big Idea

  • A food chain shows how energy passes from one living thing to the next. Remove one link and the whole ecosystem wobbles.
  • Biodiversity (lots of different species) makes nature stable. Pollution & habitat loss threaten it.
  • Conservation & sustainable development mean using resources wisely so they last for the future. ♻️
