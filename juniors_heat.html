Flat parts = latent heat (melting at 0°C, boiling at 100°C)

🧠 The Big Idea

  • Specific heat capacity: how much heat 1 kg of a substance needs to warm by 1°C. Water's is high — that's why the sea heats up slowly. 🌊
  • Latent heat: during melting & boiling the temperature stays flat even though you keep heating — the energy breaks bonds instead of raising temperature.
  • Heat energy: Q = m × c × ΔT. During a state change: Q = m × L.
