🔺 Similarity & Pythagoras

The square on the longest side equals the two smaller squares added together. See it live!

🔎 Similarity: shapes with equal angles & proportional sides are similar — same shape, different size.

🧠 The Big Idea

  • Pythagoras theorem: in a right-angled triangle, a² + b² = c² (c is the hypotenuse, the longest side).
  • Similar triangles have the same angles and sides in the same ratio — enlargements of each other.
  • These ideas let us measure the unreachable: heights of towers, widths of rivers, distances in maps. 🗺️
