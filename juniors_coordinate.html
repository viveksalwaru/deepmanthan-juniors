🧠 The Big Idea

  • Every point on the plane has an address (x, y). Geometry becomes algebra!
  • Distance formula: AB = √[(x₂−x₁)² + (y₂−y₁)²] — it's just Pythagoras in disguise. 📐
  • Midpoint is the average of the coordinates. The section formula extends this to any ratio.
