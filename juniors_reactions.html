Types of Reactions

Combination
A + B → AB  (2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O)
Decomposition
AB → A + B  (splits apart, often by heat)
Displacement
A + BC → AC + B  (a more reactive metal kicks one out)
Double Displacement
AB + CD → AD + CB  (partners swap)
Also: oxidation (gain oxygen) & reduction (loss of oxygen) · rusting = corrosion · food going stale = rancidity

🧠 The Big Idea

  • Law of conservation of mass: atoms are never created or destroyed — so a balanced equation must have the same count of each atom on both sides.
  • You balance by changing coefficients (the big numbers in front), never the small subscripts.
