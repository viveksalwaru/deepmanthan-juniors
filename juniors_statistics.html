🎲 Probability — roll a die many times

Theoretical chance of each face = 1/6 ≈ 0.167. Roll lots of times and watch reality get close!

🧠 The Big Idea

  • Mean = sum ÷ how many. Median = middle value when sorted. Mode = the value that appears most.
  • Probability = favourable outcomes ÷ total outcomes. A fair die gives each face a 1/6 chance.
  • The more you roll, the closer the experimental probability gets to the theoretical one — the Law of Large Numbers. 🔁
