🔢 Arithmetic Progression

A sequence that jumps by the same step every time. Change the start and the step and watch the pattern!

nth term: aₙ = a + (n−1)d

🧠 The Big Idea

  • An AP adds a fixed common difference d each step: a, a+d, a+2d, …
  • nth term: aₙ = a + (n−1)d. Sum of n terms: Sₙ = n/2 × [2a + (n−1)d] = n/2 × (first + last).
  • APs are everywhere — saving a fixed amount monthly, stacking rows of seats, ticking seconds. ⏱️
