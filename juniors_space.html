🧠 The Big Idea

  • A satellite is really falling toward Earth — but it moves sideways so fast that it keeps missing! That balance is a stable orbit.
  • Too slow → gravity wins → it crashes. Too fast → it beats gravity → it escapes.
  • The speed needed is the orbital velocity; the speed to break free is the escape velocity (~11.2 km/s for Earth).
