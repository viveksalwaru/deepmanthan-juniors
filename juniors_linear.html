📈 Linear Equations

Two straight lines, two equations. Where they cross is the solution! Slide the numbers and watch.

Line 1:  y = 1x + 2
Line 2:  y = -1x + -2
Solution (where they meet)
—

🧠 The Big Idea

  • Each linear equation is a straight line. Solving two together means finding the point (x, y) that lies on both.
  • One crossing → one unique solution. Parallel lines → no solution. Same line → infinitely many solutions.
  • You can solve them by graphing, substitution or elimination — the graph shows why the answer works.
