🧠 The Big Idea

  • In a vacuum (no air), everything falls at the same rate — the feather and hammer land together! (Astronauts proved this on the Moon. 🌙)
  • On Earth, air resistance slows light, fluffy things — that's the only reason a feather drifts down.
  • Acceleration due to gravity g ≈ 9.8 m/s². Free fall: v = gt, h = ½gt².
  • Mass = how much matter (kg, unchanging). Weight = pull of gravity = m × g (newtons, changes with g).
