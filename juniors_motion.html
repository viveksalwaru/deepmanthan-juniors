🧠 The Big Idea

  • Uniform speed → distance–time graph is a straight slanted line; velocity–time is flat.
  • Speeding up (acceleration) → distance–time curves upward; velocity–time slants up.
  • The steeper the distance–time line, the faster the car is going.
  • Equations of motion: v = u + at  ·  s = ut + ½at²  ·  v² = u² + 2as
